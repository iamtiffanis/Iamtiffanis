The CRAFT method

In the session we'll

  • CCalibrate: choose your one bold move for the next 90 days and why it matters to you
  • RRelease: name the self-doubt, guilt or "shoulds" that keep stalling you
  • AActivate: break the move into small 30, 60 and 90 day steps
  • FFeel: build the routines and rest that keep your energy up
  • TTrust: set a simple way to check in and adjust instead of starting over
