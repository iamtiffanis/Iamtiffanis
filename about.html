About Tiffani

It doesn't feel like starting over anymore.

It feels like everything I've learned finally coming together, for the same mission I've always had.

Where it started

Becoming a mom in 2016 opened my eyes to a whole world I didn't know existed: self-employment. I wanted to be home with my babies and still make my own money, so I jumped in the way a lot of us do, with one MLM, and then another.

Those didn't last, but something else did. I fell in love with learning how to support women, and I started chasing certifications and deeper understanding: holistic birth doula, vaginal steam facilitation, Usui reiki, self-love coaching, womb reiki and restorative yoga.

Along the way, I never stopped making things. I've been an artist and sold my art and wire-wrapped crystal jewelry, so I know what it's like to pour your heart into something and then figure out how to share it and sell it.

Every brand, one mission

I've rebranded more times than I can count. Injoymint. Blissful Mamas Birth & Wellness. Mama Malachite. Empress Healing Arts. And a few more in between.

The names changed, but my mission never did: helping women create healthier lifestyles and make income on their terms, so they can do whatever they want. Stay home with their kids. Travel. Leave toxic relationships. All while helping others.

Learning the business side

After years of studying how to start a healing business, I noticed something. So many amazing women offer these services, or want to, and they struggle with all the ideas the same way I have. They aren't short on gifts. They're short on structure.

That led me to project management certification, and now to training as a digital business manager. Working in partnership fulfillment and event operations, I've gotten to do the behind-the-scenes business work I spent years learning about: timelines, systems, partners and live events.

What I do now

I'm an energy healer and digital business manager for multi-passionate mystic women. I bring both sides together:

  • Energy healing through reiki, astrology, Human Design and coaching, so you can see how you're wired and work with your energy instead of against it
  • Strategic, practical support through DBM services, so your ideas have systems that can hold them

I'm here for the creatives, entrepreneurs, healers and multi-passionates who care deeply and have so much potential, but struggle to connect it all together. Whether that's one service or multiple streams of income that work together, we'll build something that fits you.

Certified self-love coach, womb and Usui reiki practitioner, holistic birth doula, vaginal steam facilitator, restorative yoga teacher and project manager.
